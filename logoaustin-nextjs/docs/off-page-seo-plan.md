# Off-page SEO action plan (ethical, to be executed after launch)

Website development does not complete off-page SEO. The items below are recommendations for the business owner or an authorised marketing partner; none have been carried out.

## 1. Google Business Profile (highest priority)
- Confirm ownership of the "Logo Design Austin / Logo in Hours" listing shown in the footer map; make the NAP identical to the website (`7600 Chevy Chase Dr Suite 300, Austin, TX 78752`, `(512) 782-9748`), category "Graphic designer" / "Logo design", service area list matching the site.
- Add the services list (custom logo, AI logo cleanup, branding, graphic design, website design), hours, booking link to `/consultation/`, and 10–20 portfolio photos with descriptive captions.
- Ask every completed client for a Google review via a direct review link; reply to each review. Verified reviews can then be added to `src/data/reviews.ts` with source links.
- Post monthly updates (new logos, offers) to the profile.

## 2. Citations and directories
- Core: Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, BBB, Yellow Pages, Nextdoor Business, Facebook page (already linked), LinkedIn company page.
- Industry: Clutch, DesignRush, UpCity, Behance/Dribbble portfolio profiles (link back to the portfolio page), 99designs-style marketplaces only if the business already uses them.
- Local: Austin Chamber of Commerce, Greater Austin Hispanic Chamber (fits the bilingual audience), Round Rock / Cedar Park / Georgetown chambers for the location pages.
- Keep name, address, phone and URL identical everywhere; track in a spreadsheet.

## 3. Digital PR and editorial mentions
- Pitch "before/after: fixing AI-generated logos" stories to Austin business publications (Austin Business Journal, Culturemap, Austin Inno) and small-business newsletters; the `/fix-ai-generated-logo/` page is the natural landing page.
- Offer free logo audits to local startup programs (Capital Factory, SCORE Austin, Austin SBDC) in exchange for a resource-page mention.
- Contribute guest articles to printing partners, sign shops and embroidery vendors (the file-format and DST content is genuinely useful to their customers).

## 4. Partnerships
- Reciprocal "preferred partner" listings with Austin print shops, sign companies, web hosts and photographers who already receive Logo Austin files.
- Co-marketing with the $950 Business Starter Package: accountants, business-formation services and coworking spaces that onboard new businesses.

## 5. Reputation management
- Monitor brand mentions (Google Alerts for "Logo Austin", "Logo In Hours").
- Respond to all reviews; resolve negative ones offline first.
- Never buy reviews or links; never publish self-authored reviews.

## 6. Content distribution
- Share each new guide on Facebook, LinkedIn and the Google profile; repurpose portfolio pieces into short case notes once clients approve naming them (feeds the portfolio detail pages planned in `architecture-and-plans.md`).

## Explicitly excluded
Purchased links, private blog networks, link exchanges at scale, fabricated reviews or testimonials, doorway pages.
